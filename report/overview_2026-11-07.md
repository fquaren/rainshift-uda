## 1. What the project is

This is a diagnosis paper on unsupervised domain adaptation (UDA) for precipitation
super-resolution, ERA5 → IMERG, on three RainShift regions. It is not a method paper. The
organising thesis, from the 2026-10-28 literature review: conflicting reports of cross-region
transfer in downscaling sort by one property of the design, whether P(Y|X) is shared across
regions by construction. Perfect-model and same-product designs (Qian 2026, Singh 2026,
CORDEX-ML-Bench, our own COSMO work in EGU26-19396) share the conditional, so transfer and UDA
work. Reanalysis-to-observation designs (RainShift, Harder et al. 2512.01400) do not share it,
transfer fails, and unlabelled target data cannot fix that.

Scope guard (PLAN.md "Out of scope"): if the work turns into making AFM or a UDA method beat
source-only, it has drifted into a method paper with no contribution behind it.

Target venue: undecided. Proposed AIES (AMS); TMLR if identifiability becomes the headline; a
workshop version (e.g. Tackling Climate Change with ML) to stake the claim early.

## 3. Setup

**Data.** RainShift. ERA5 inputs at 80×80 → IMERG target at 200×200 (0.1°, ~2000 km), hourly.
Domains: `europe_west`, `horn-of-africa`, `melanesia` (`blacksea` is a dummy). 11 inputs: 9
dynamic (cape, tcw, tclw, cp, tp, u700, v700, sp, tisr) and 2 static (lsm, z). Train zarr
175,320 steps, test 17,520 (87 full chunks plus a 120-step partial), chunked 200 in time. The
validation split is 88 strided chunks with purge gap 1, at offset `42 % 9 = 6` for every seed,
leaving 613 train chunks = **122,520** train samples.

**Transform.** log(P·1000 + 1e-5), RainShift's own, z-scored **with the source's statistics for
every dataset, target included**. Precipitation s_S is 3.72 (europe), 2.70 (horn) and 4.68
(melanesia). `mse_std` is therefore in s_S² units, and cross-row subtractions must use
`mse_log` = `mse_std` × s_S².

**Headline metric (D1, 2026-10-29).** `mse_log_e01` = mean (log(P̂+0.1) − log(P+0.1))², with P̂
clipped at 0. Robustness rows: 0.01 mm/h and the training space. Literature basis: GSDR-I,
Harris 2022, Glawion 2025 and IMERG–MRMS validation all use 0.1 mm/h; Santos 2018 shows that
log scores are oversensitive to ε. AFM headline: `crps_log_e01`, fair CRPS, M = 8, 10 midpoint
steps. `rmse_mm` and `crps_mm` are recorded but not quoted, because isolated pixels blow up in
mm.

**Models.**
- UNet: `DualEncoderUNet`, width 32, batch 128, LR 5e-4 (trainer default, never tuned). Phase 1
  uses patience 5; phase 2 also uses 5 per the configs (PLAN.md's "−1" was wrong, audit A6); the
  oracle uses 8.
- AFM (adaptive flow matching, Fotiadis et al. 2025): encoder identical to the UNet (width 32),
  batch 64, LR 1e-4, encoder ratio 1.0, EMA 0.999, selection on validation CRPS. Width 64 OOMs on
  the A100s and will not be revisited, because it would confound capacity with objective.

**Protocol.** Phase 1: source-only per domain; the diagonal runs double as the in-domain models
h_T. Oracle: `--uda_method none --joint_training`, kept apart only by directory (`*_oracle/`).
Phase 2: UDA methods on 6 ordered pairs. Decomposition: gap = source-only − in-domain;
λ̂ = oracle − in-domain on target (`lambda_tgt`); `lambda_joint` adds the oracle's source cost;
budget = source-only − oracle; recovery = (source-only − method)/budget.

**Compute and stacks.** Phase 1, the oracles and JDOT (seeds 42–44) ran on GH200 with the
container (torch 2.9). The other phase-2 methods and everything since 2026-09-30 ran on A100
with dl-torch (torch 2.13). The stack check (`unet_seed42_a100check/`) agrees in training loss to
1e-4, an order of magnitude below seed spread. Its test metrics were never scored (P8). A100 is
1.8× slower; a UNet phase-2 run costs about 1.83 A100-h.

### 5.3 GAP ablation (europe→melanesia, MMD, 4 arms × 3 seeds, 2026-10-06)

Read only in `mse_std` (1e-5 floor), never re-scored at D1 (audit A9).
- tgtfwd (target forward, no alignment): reproduces the methods' source inflation. **Rule 3
  holds: it is BatchNorm mixing** (ratios 0.91 / 1.21 / 0.96).
- gap: does nothing against source-only (mean −0.05, sd 0.63).
- grid4: −0.39 / −0.40 / −0.46 on target at Δsrc +2.57, a trade the size of the oracle budget.
- grid4ms: no effect.
- Rule 2's "moves target risk" rests on a bad seed-42 control. Rule 1 passes trivially, because
  the unbiased MMD is negative.
- Supported wording: GAP alignment is inert; spatially resolved alignment trades source for
  target.

## 7. Standing caveats for the methods/limitations section

- Seed error bars are initialisation variance only: `--seed` does not reach the dataset, and
  data order also repeats every epoch.
- Two software/hardware stacks line up with the method split (GH200: phase 1, oracle, JDOT;
  A100: the rest). The test-metric check (P8) is not done.
- UNet LR untuned, AFM LR tuned. That is the conservative direction for claim 4.
- Source and target batches are time-locked (A7).
- Every log-floor result needs a mm bias / wet-fraction / max row beside it.
- Feature losses in AFM reach only the shared encoder, never the flow net. Spectral acts on μ
  only.
- AFM AdaBN mixes a BatchNorm re-estimation cost (up to 0.015 CRPS) with adaptation.

### 9.1 AFM phase 2 with a target forward is broken by BatchNorm, not by alignment

The methods are joint_ot, spectral and MMD gr0.5. All three fail in **the same three pairs**:
horn→europe, melanesia→europe and melanesia→horn. In each case the source validation CRPS is best
at epoch 1 and then climbs: joint_ot to 2.2–3.0, spectral to 2.1–2.7, MMD to 2.7–4.2. The scored
checkpoint is therefore an epoch-1 model. In horn→europe, for all three methods, it is the
all-zero predictor on both source and target: target `crps_log` is 0.2041, the zero forecast's
value; source `crps_mm` is 0.0548, horn's field mean.

The cause is BatchNorm running statistics, not the alignment loss. The evidence is strong but
circumstantial:
- The *training* losses of these runs track phase 1 to the third decimal (flow 0.286 / 0.277
  against phase 1's 0.285 / 0.275 at epochs 3–4) while the eval-mode validation diverges. That
  split between batch statistics and running statistics is BatchNorm's signature.
- Spectral's alignment term is about 0.007, negligible against the task loss, and it fails
  exactly like joint_ot and MMD.
- FDA has no target forward and trains normally in all six pairs.
- The target forward runs in train mode with BatchNorm momentum 0.1, after the source forward
  (`train_afm.py:213–238`; spectral passes only through the encoder). The running statistics
  used at evaluation are therefore a source/target mixture, the "BatchNorm mixing" that GAP
  rule 3 found in the UNet arm.
- The pairs that fail all have a horn or melanesia source. A plausible reason, not checked:
  narrow tropical input statistics put the target far out in source-normalised units.
- Even the runs that "train" are damaged. Their source validation CRPS is 0.29–0.47, against
  phase-1 values of 0.217 (horn) and 0.261 (europe). joint_ot fails in 5 of 6 pairs; only
  horn→melanesia escapes, and its target is near-zero there.

**This corrects PLAN.md's Nov 4 reading of MMD gr0.5** ("it is the alignment, not only the
transient"). The same divergence happens with spectral, where the alignment weight is negligible.

**Consequence:** joint_ot, spectral and MMD rows in the AFM arm say nothing about claim 3 as
trained. They measure BatchNorm mixing, which is itself a finding for the GAP-rule-3 story.

**Check that needs no retraining:** re-estimate the *encoder and flow-net* running statistics on
source-only training batches for a non-diverged run, e.g. spectral europe→melanesia (source val
0.466), and re-score source validation CRPS. If it falls to about 0.26–0.30, the diagnosis is
confirmed. The diverged runs saved only their epoch-1 `best.pt`, so they cannot be probed this
way. `afm_val_diag.py` already re-estimates the flow net, but not the encoder.

**A fix is a design choice, not a bug fix.** Options: run the target pass without updating
running statistics, or use domain-specific BatchNorm. Either makes the AFM arm differ from the
UNet arm.

### 9.2 AFM AdaBN: harmful everywhere

Source scores are phase 1's by construction.
- Target `crps_log` is worse than source-only in all six pairs. Examples: europe→horn 0.1125
  against 0.0933; melanesia→europe 0.2876 against 0.1947.
- Four of six pairs blow up in mm: ensemble spread 2.7–11.2 mm, `crps_mm` 0.53–4.69, against
  field means of 0.05–0.31.
- This is consistent with 9.1, where target-domain BatchNorm statistics are toxic for this
  model, and with the UNet AdaBN harming 5 of 6 pairs.
